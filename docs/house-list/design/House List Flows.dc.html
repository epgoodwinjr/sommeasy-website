The House List

A wine list, written for one — every page of it.

Home as chosen, carried through matching a list, logging a bottle, the journal and a wine’s own page, search, your palate, and the account pages. Every phone below is the whole prototype opened to a different page — start anywhere and tap through. Reset puts it back.

Entries, not cards

Actions, wines and settings are lines of type that run on leader dots to a target, a price or a value.

Numbered sections

Roman numerals and small-caps heads do the work panels used to. Hairlines are the only dividers.

A crest on every page

Home keeps the full masthead. Inner pages get a slim running head: back, the protea for home, and search.

Two actions everywhere

Inner pages keep Match a list and Log a bottle in a bar along the bottom. Turn it off in Tweaks to compare.
I.

The list

Home as chosen. Search and your account now sit either side of the wordmark, and the masthead opens your palate. Last night’s verdict and every selection are answered in place.
II.

Tonight, out

Bringing the list is one tap. The filters become Tonight’s terms, one ruled line each. Picks are set like a list’s by-the-bottle page — role, name, price, then the Somm’s note — and choosing one quiets the rest.
III.

Tonight, in

Snap the label, search, or log something from your want list. The verdict is four lines to choose from. Saving shows what the rating did — here, a second loved Ken Forrester puts the estate in your DNA.
IV.

Journal

Grouped by month like a cellar book, each bottle ending on its verdict. A wine’s page holds your verdict, the Somm’s note, what it changed in your palate, and its particulars on leader lines.
V.

Search & your palate

One search across your journal, your DNA and the Somm’s suggestions. Your palate sets the signature as three hairline rails and the DNA as an index with bottle counts; ✦ marks what your ratings earned.
VI.

Account

Fields are ruled lines, and the primary action is the same leader-and-circle entry as home. Settings read as terms with their values on the right.
